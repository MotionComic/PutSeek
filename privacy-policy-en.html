PutSeek Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026

This is the privacy policy for PutSeek on iPhone. PutSeek remembers, on the device, where you put things, when food expires, and items you don’t want others to see.

10226093@qq.com

Summary

While you use PutSeek, the app does not connect to the network to send your items, food, or speech. There is no account, and there is no server that receives those records. The developer cannot see what you saved in the app.

What stays on this iPhone

Item names, places, food names, expiry dates, other names, and earlier places that you say or type are stored in the app’s local storage and are not synced to iCloud. Hidden items are kept in a separate local store on this iPhone, with iCloud sync turned off. To open them, iOS checks Face ID, Touch ID, or the device passcode. PutSeek only learns whether the check succeeded. It does not receive or store your face, fingerprint, or passcode. If you allow notifications, the app schedules expiry reminders on the device. The reminder text may include a food name and a date. iOS shows it on this iPhone. It is not sent to the developer.

System permissions

The microphone is used only to hear where you put things and when food expires. Speech recognition turns that sentence into text on this iPhone. PutSeek does not upload the audio or the transcript. The first time you dictate in a language, iOS may download that language’s offline speech components. That download is performed by Apple’s system, not by PutSeek sending your recording. Notifications are local reminders for food that is nearing its date. Face ID is used only to open Hidden.

What we don’t do

PutSeek does not show ads, does not include third-party analytics or tracking, does not sell personal information, and does not give your items, food, or speech to third parties. The app does not read your contacts, photos, or GPS location.

Email you choose to send

If you write to 10226093@qq.com, we receive your email address and whatever you include, and we use it only to reply. Please don’t include home locations or item details unless you want us to see them. The message is handled by your mail provider and by the mailbox that receives it.

How long it is kept

Records stay on the device until you delete them in the app or delete PutSeek. Deleting the app removes its local data from that iPhone. Support emails are kept long enough to handle the question, and then according to the mailbox’s ordinary retention.

Children

PutSeek is not directed at children under 13, and it does not collect children’s information through a server.

This website

This page is static. It does not set cookies or include analytics, and it does not receive anything you saved in the app. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. The host may keep ordinary access logs under its own policy.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes in a material way, we will update this page and the date above. Privacy questions: 10226093@qq.com.
