PutSeek Support

This is the support page for PutSeek on iPhone. It remembers where things are, when food expires, and can keep items you don’t want others to see in Hidden.

10226093@qq.com

We reply by email. You don’t need to include item names or places in your home unless you want us to read them.

Getting started

  1. The first time you hold to talk, allow the microphone and speech recognition.
  2. Hold the button, say one complete sentence, and let go.
  3. Hold again when you want to ask. If it didn’t catch you, switch to typing.

Where is my data?

Items, food, and what you said stay on this iPhone. PutSeek has no account and does not upload them.

Do I need a network?

Not while you use the app. The first time you dictate in a language, iOS may prepare offline speech on this iPhone. That is a system download. After that, it works without a network.

What are the microphone and speech recognition for?

The microphone is only used to hear where you put things and when food expires. Speech is turned into text on this iPhone and is not uploaded.

What are the expiry reminders?

If you allow notifications, the iPhone reminds you on the device when food is getting close to its date. The text may include the food name and the date, and it appears only on this iPhone. You can turn off PutSeek notifications in iOS Settings.

How do I open Hidden?

With this iPhone’s Face ID, Touch ID, or passcode. Set a device passcode in Settings first. PutSeek does not receive your face or fingerprint, and it does not ask for a separate password.

How do I delete records?

Delete an item from its details. Deleting the app also removes the local records on this iPhone.

Which languages?

Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, Japanese, and Spanish. Change this in the app’s settings.
